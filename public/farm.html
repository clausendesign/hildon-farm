ABOUT THIS MAP

A familiar place.
A different perspective.

A first interactive model of the family farm on Sandon Road, near Feilding.

Terrain
LINZ / Horizons Regional Council LiDAR, surveyed 2015–2016. The displayed mesh is sampled from the measured ground surface.
Aerial photography
Open aerial photography supplied through LINZ.
Farm outline
Traced from your marked screenshot and aligned with aerial landmarks. It is approximate, not a surveyed legal boundary.
Water paths
Paths follow the steepest descent on the sampled terrain. This is a visual exploration of surface flow, not a rainfall, drainage or flood prediction.
Terrain relief
The default 2× height exaggeration makes subtle changes in the land easier to see. Set it to 1× for true horizontal-to-vertical scale.
